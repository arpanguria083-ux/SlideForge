# SlideForge AI - GDPR Compliance & Global Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 25, 2026

The SlideForge AI project ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines how SlideForge AI ("Software"), a desktop-based application running on Windows, handles personal data in strict compliance with the **General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR")**, the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR"), and other applicable global data privacy frameworks.

---

## 1. Information About the Data Controller

SlideForge AI operates under a **local-first and offline-first architectural model**. This means that under ordinary operational circumstances, the Software runs entirely on your local Windows system, and **no personal data, document files, or API keys are transmitted to us or stored on our servers.**

## 2. Our Local-First & Privacy-By-Design Processing Model

1. **Local Isolation:** All slide documents (PDFs, PPTXs), structural analyses, visual coordinates, audit checklists, review histories, and local configurations are stored exclusively on your own Windows computer. We do not host, capture, or inspect your documents.
2. **Privacy-by-Design (Art. 25 GDPR):** SlideForge AI was engineered to minimize data processing. You maintain sole and exclusive ownership, access, and custody over all raw inputs and outputs generated by the Software.
3. **No Centralized Servers:** We do not operate a centralized cloud database for document processing, indexing, or LLM hosting.

---

## 3. Categories of Data Processed Locally

To run SlideForge AI, the Software processes the following categories of data, all of which remain located on your Windows endpoint:

1. **Document Content:** Slide decks (PDF/PPTX), text strings, tables, images, and visual elements extracted from the documents you choose to analyze.
2. **Application Configuration:** Local settings, interface language choice, user role selections (e.g., Junior or Senior reviewer), and model choices (e.g., Ollama, LM Studio, OpenAI).
3. **API Keys & Credentials:** If you choose to use remote cloud LLM providers, your custom API keys (e.g., OpenAI API key) are stored encrypted or in local configuration files on your system.
4. **Diagnostic and Error Logs:** Local text-based logs containing execution timestamps, hardware acceleration capabilities, error codes, and local backend connection state (e.g., PyTorch initialization logs).
5. **Session Data:** Temporary session databases containing historical annotations, cached OCR bounding boxes, and workspace history records.

## 4. Legal Bases for Processing (Article 6 GDPR)

Because all data processing occurs locally on your machine, you are the controller of your own data lifecycle. However, to the extent that the Software acts as the processing instrument on your behalf, the legal bases under GDPR are:

1. **Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR):** Processing is necessary to provide the slide review and editing features you initiate when using the application.
2. **Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR):** Processing local diagnostic logs, configuration flags, and system checks is necessary to secure the stability, optimization, and correct execution of the Software on your Windows hardware.
3. **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR):** If we implement any opt-in diagnostic sharing or feedback features in future releases, they will operate strictly on an opt-in basis, and you can withdraw your consent at any time.

## 5. Third-Party Integrations & International Data Transfers

SlideForge AI allows you to configure external model providers. It is critical to understand the privacy implications of these selections:

### A. Local Providers (Ollama, LM Studio, local Surya OCR)
* **Privacy Impact:** Data remains **100% offline** on your local machine or local area network. No data leaves your system, and there are no international transfers under GDPR.

### B. Remote Cloud Providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, etc.)
* **Privacy Impact:** If you configure remote API models, the Software will transmit the text and layout coordinates of your slides directly from your machine to the third-party API endpoint.
* **Independent Controllers:** These third-party companies act as independent Data Controllers. They will process your data according to their own Privacy Policies and Terms of Service.
* **International Transfers (Art. 44-49 GDPR):** Transmitting data to US-based providers (like OpenAI) constitutes an international transfer. You are responsible for ensuring that your use of these services complies with lawful transfer mechanisms (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses - SCCs) and your organization's internal compliance policies.

## 6. Cookies and Local Storage

SlideForge AI is a desktop application wrapped in an Electron shell. It does not use traditional tracking cookies or commercial ad beacons. 

The application utilizes standard web storage APIs available in the Electron window to store local operational state:
1. **HTML5 LocalStorage / IndexedDB:** Used to store your accepted terms flag, UI theme selections, active session IDs, and active reviewer role.
2. **Filesystem Storage:** Used to write local database configurations and cached slide assets into `%AppData%\slideforge-ai\` or `%LocalAppData%\Programs\slideforge-ai\`.

You can clear these assets at any time by selecting "Clear History" in the application dashboard, or by deleting the cache files directly from your Windows User profile directory.

## 7. Your Data Subject Rights Under GDPR

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights. Because SlideForge AI processes your data locally, you can exercise most of these rights directly and autonomously:

1. **Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR):** You have the right to access all data processed by the Software. You can view all processed annotations and files directly in the application's dashboard or by inspecting the files in `%AppData%\slideforge-ai\`.
2. **Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR):** You can correct any inaccurate configuration data, role pickers, or API settings directly inside the application settings panel.
3. **Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17 GDPR):** You can delete all your data, slide assets, and history. To do this, click the "Close Session / Delete History" button in the application interface, or delete the folders `%AppData%\slideforge-ai\` and `%LocalAppData%\slideforge-ai-backend\`.
4. **Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR):** You can restrict data processing by switching the active AI model to "Offline Mode" or using local offline engines like Ollama, preventing any network transmission.
5. **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR):** You can export your data and slide analysis tables as standard structured files directly from the dashboard.
6. **Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR):** You can object to local processing by closing the application or uninstalling it using the Windows Control Panel.
7. **Right to Lodge a Complaint (Art. 77 GDPR):** You have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent Data Protection Supervisory Authority in the EU or UK if you believe that the software's execution violates data privacy laws.

## 8. Data Retention and Security

1. **Data Retention:** We do not retain your data because we do not collect it. Your local slide records, session databases, and audit logs are retained on your Windows machine for as long as you choose. They are deleted when you manually clear the application cache or uninstall the program.
2. **Data Security:** SlideForge AI implements local sandboxing rules through Electron and restricts IPC (Inter-Process Communication) pathways. However, because data resides on your machine, the security of your data depends on your Windows endpoint controls, local network security, firewall rules, and password protections.
